About BlinkDeal

Gold deals, unlocked in a blink.

Online gold deals are short. A Myntra BlinkDeal can be over in ten minutes, and a good gold coin sells out faster than that. Questions

What is BlinkDeal?
A free front door to four gold deal boards – Myntra, Amazon, Flipkart and Ajio. Tap a store on the wheel and you land on a board that lists that store's gold coins and jewellery, each priced against today's gold rate.
What is a Myntra BlinkDeal?
A short flash coupon Myntra runs on its gold coin listings, usually for about ten minutes, with codes such as BLINKDEAL6 or BLINKDEAL8 that take a fixed percentage off the listed price. Myntra allows one coupon per order, and during a BlinkDeal that coupon is the deal itself.
How do the boards decide if a gold coin is a good deal?
Each piece's price after the store's coupon and offers is compared with the value of the gold in it, from its weight and purity at a reference gold rate you choose. You see what you pay, what the gold is worth, and the gap between them. Nothing is hidden – every piece stays on the list.
Where does the gold rate come from?
From Karat Board, which publishes the daily 22K and 24K gold rates of leading Indian jewellers. You can pick which jeweller's rate to compare against.
Is BlinkDeal free? Do I need an account?
Yes, it is free, and there is nothing to sign up for. Open a board, look, and buy on the store's own site if you choose to.
Is this investment advice?
No. BlinkDeal shows data points – price, gold content and the gap between them. Whether to buy is your call. Buy links on the boards may be affiliate links; a small commission may be earned at no extra cost to you.
